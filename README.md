# To be or not to be (2B-N2B)

To be or not to be (2B-N2B) is a minimalistic, no bloat, to-do list manager.

## How is it different from all other to-do list managers?

Because of Prof. Coco. Professor Coco has mastered all languages, arithemetic operations known to man(and cats), all physics, and all other subject that you can think of. He even started his own branch of science to figure out the phenomenon of why cats have 9 lives. Prof. Coco will assist you in completing all your tasks while encouraging you and giving tips.
